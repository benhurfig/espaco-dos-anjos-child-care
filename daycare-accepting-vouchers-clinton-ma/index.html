Voucher acceptance

Massachusetts Child Care Vouchers Accepted

Espaço dos Anjos Child Care accepts Massachusetts Child Care Vouchers. Families who use a voucher can share that information when they begin a Family Request.

Daycare acceptance is separate from the eligibility, approval and placement procedures that may apply to each family. A voucher does not guarantee child care availability or enrollment.

A simple first step

Share Your Voucher Information in the Family Request

The Family Request gives parents a simple way to introduce their family and describe their child care needs. If you use a Massachusetts Child Care Voucher, you can mention it before Espaço dos Anjos contacts you about the next steps.

This page does not determine voucher eligibility or approval. It confirms that Espaço dos Anjos accepts vouchers as a form of child care assistance.
