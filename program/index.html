OUR PROGRAMS

Learning through play, creativity and discovery.

Every activity is thoughtfully designed to encourage confidence, curiosity and meaningful childhood development.

Early Learning

Letters, numbers and language introduced through fun, age-appropriate activities.

Arts & Crafts

Daily creative projects that inspire imagination, expression and confidence.

Problem Solving

Games and activities that strengthen memory, reasoning and critical thinking.

Outdoor Play

Social Skills

Music & Movement

DAILY ROUTINE

A balanced day filled with learning, play and care.

Every day follows a consistent routine that helps children feel safe, confident and excited to learn through hands-on experiences.

7:30 AM

Welcome & Arrival

Children arrive, settle in and enjoy a calm start to the day.

8:00 AM

Free Play

Children explore, socialize and choose age-appropriate activities.

8:30 AM

Morning Snack

Hand washing followed by a healthy morning snack.

9:30 AM

Circle Time

Songs, stories, calendar, language and group learning activities.

10:00 AM

Learning Activities

Hands-on educational activities including arts, crafts, letters and numbers.

10:30 AM

Outdoor Play

Outdoor activities that encourage movement, exploration and teamwork.

11:30 AM

Lunch Time

Hand washing followed by a nutritious homemade lunch.

12:30 PM

Brush Teeth

Children practice healthy hygiene habits after lunch.

1:00 PM

Nap & Quiet Time

Rest time helps children recharge for the afternoon.

3:30 PM

Afternoon Snack

Healthy snacks shared together before afternoon activities.

4:30 PM

Music & Movement

Music, dancing and movement activities that encourage confidence and coordination.

5:00 PM

Goodbye & Pick-up

Children prepare to go home after a fun and enriching day.

A typical day, not a rigid timetable. The rhythm can be adapted to the children’s ages and needs. Child care hours are Monday–Friday, 7:30 AM–5:30 PM.

HOW CHILDREN LEARN

Real Learning. Real Play. Real Childhood.

We believe children learn best through hands-on activities, creativity, movement and meaningful interactions in a safe and nurturing environment.

Hands-on Learning

Art, crafts, sensory activities and educational games that inspire curiosity and creativity.

Stories & Language

Books, songs and conversations that build communication, vocabulary and early literacy skills.

Outdoor Play

Daily outdoor activities that encourage movement, exploration and teamwork.

Screen-Free Learning

Children spend their day learning through play, creativity and meaningful interactions instead of screen time.

Care beyond activities

Meals, rest and a welcoming space matter too.

A child’s day is more than a list of activities. Shared meals, quiet time and an organized environment help create a familiar place to return to.
