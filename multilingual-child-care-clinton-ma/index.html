What Multilingual Child Care Means at Espaço dos Anjos

English, Portuguese and Spanish are available at Espaço dos Anjos. This multilingual environment helps families ask questions, explain their child's needs and understand important information in the language they feel most comfortable using.

Multilingual describes communication and the everyday environment. Espaço dos Anjos is not presented as a language school, immersion program or formal bilingual or trilingual curriculum.

Communication with families

Communication That Helps Families Feel Welcome

Choosing child care often comes with important questions. Families may want to discuss routines, ages, schedules, care needs and what happens after they submit their information.

Being able to communicate in a familiar language can make those conversations clearer and help families feel more comfortable while learning about the Child Care.

Three languages available

English, Portuguese and Spanish Are Available

Families can communicate in English, Portuguese or Spanish when sharing their child care needs. This does not mean that every activity is provided equally in all three languages or that the program offers formal language instruction.

Learning throughout the day

Everyday Learning Through Stories, Music and Play

The daily environment includes stories, conversations, music and movement, arts and crafts, sensory experiences, letters and numbers, outdoor play and other hands-on, age-appropriate activities.

These are everyday child care and learning experiences. They are not presented as a formal multilingual curriculum or a promise that children will become fluent in a language.

Questions About Our Multilingual Environment

What languages are available?

English, Portuguese and Spanish are available for communication with families.

Is Espaço dos Anjos a language school or immersion program?
