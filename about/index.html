The person behind the care

A home where each child is known.

Luciene is the owner and educator at Espaço dos Anjos Child Care. Her degree in Pedagogy supports a thoughtful approach to early learning, while the family child care setting makes space for personal attention and a close relationship with families.

Care and learning happen together: through stories, play, creative activities, shared meals and steady routines. Our goal is a place where children feel respected, supported and encouraged to explore at their own pace.

“Every child deserves a place where they feel safe, loved and excited to learn.”Luciene · Espaço dos Anjos Child Care
A thoughtful foundation

What shapes our everyday care

Licensed family care

A licensed Family Child Care in Clinton for children from 6 months to 5 years.

An educator’s perspective

Luciene brings her Pedagogy background to age-appropriate activities and the small moments that help children learn.

Small-group attention

A familiar setting creates opportunities to notice each child’s interests, comfort and day-to-day needs.

Working with families

The relationship starts before the first day.

Feeling comfortable with your child care provider matters. We begin by learning about your family, your child and the care you are looking for.

Children hear English and Portuguese through everyday interactions, songs and stories. Play-based experiences, a welcoming environment and communication with families are central to our approach.
