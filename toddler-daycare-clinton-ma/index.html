Care as children grow

Care for Growing Children in Clinton

Young children are busy exploring, moving, listening, creating and participating in the world around them. Espaço dos Anjos offers a Family Child Care setting where experiences are selected with children's ages in mind.

The business serves the confirmed age range of 6 months to 5 years. “Toddler daycare” describes what many families search for; it is not the name of a separate classroom or administrative program at Espaço dos Anjos.

Activities and experiences

Age-Appropriate Activities and Everyday Experiences

Depending on the day and the children's ages, experiences may include the documented activities below.

Stories
Opportunities to listen to stories and take part in everyday conversations.
Music & Movement
Music and movement experiences that invite children to participate.
Arts
Creative, hands-on opportunities using art materials.
Sensory Experiences
Age-appropriate ways to explore through the senses.
Letters & Numbers
Everyday contact with letters and numbers without a formal academic promise.
Outdoor Play
Time for outdoor play, movement and exploration when appropriate.
Ask About Child Care
Learning in everyday moments

Stories, Music, Art and Sensory Experiences

Children can encounter stories, music and movement, arts, sensory experiences, letters and numbers, and practical everyday activities as part of the child care environment.

These experiences offer ways to explore, play, create and participate. They are not presented as a formal toddler curriculum or as a guarantee of specific learning or developmental outcomes.

Space to move and explore

Outdoor Play and Movement

Outdoor play and movement are among the experiences available at Espaço dos Anjos. The outdoor area provides a real child care space where children can move and explore when those activities are appropriate.

A Family Child Care setting

A Small Group in a Licensed Child Care

Espaço dos Anjos is licensed by the Massachusetts EEC and operates as a small-group Family Child Care in Clinton. Luciene, the owner, has a Degree in Pedagogy.

Learn more about Luciene and the child care on the Espaço dos Anjos home page.
